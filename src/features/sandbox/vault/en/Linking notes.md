# Linking notes

Two square brackets link to another note: [[Welcome]]. Type `[[` and Carbon suggests notes as you type their name. Ctrl+click a link to follow it, or rest the pointer on it to see the note.

## Different words for a link

A link can show other words than the name of the note: [[Tags|sorting with tags]].

## Links to a heading or a block

- To a heading: [[Writing#Tables]]
- To a block, a paragraph with an id at its end: [[#^idea]]

A link is only as good as the thought behind it. ^idea

## Notes that do not exist yet

A link to a note that is not written, such as [[Someday]], is drawn faded. Follow it to create the note.

## Backlinks and the graph

The right panel shows the outline of a note, the notes that link to it, and the graph of the notes around it. Open it with the button in the top right corner, or Ctrl+Shift+B.

Renaming a note changes every link to it, in every note.
