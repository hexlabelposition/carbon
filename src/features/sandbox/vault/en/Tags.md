# Tags

A word after `#` is a tag: #idea, #reading. Tags sort notes without moving them into folders, and a note can have as many as it needs.

A slash nests tags: #project/carbon and #project/garden both belong to #project.

Tags can also be listed at the top of a note, in its properties: this note has `sandbox` and `carbon/tour` there.

The tags view in the toolbar on the left lists every tag of the vault, with how many notes have it. Click a tag to find its notes.
